One front page summing up Saturday 21 November 2026: the Shenzhen peace push
(a US deal by January, Zelensky's yes to Christmas talks), missiles falling on
Poland, tanker strikes at a wartime peak in the Strait of Hormuz, the ICJ's
Myanmar genocide verdict, COP31 deadlocked in overtime, and the AI sell-off
dragging stocks into correction.

`main.py` decodes `image.b64` and writes the 1024×1024 front page to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is drawn procedurally with PIL from the
verified headline set in `BUILD_NOTES.md`.
